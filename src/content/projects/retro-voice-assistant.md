Talking to an LLM through a tiny speaker in an old case. The hardware is M5Stack's [ATOM Echo](https://shop.m5stack.com/products/atom-echo-smart-speaker-dev-kit) (and the AtomS3R with the Atomic Echo Base), which put a microphone, speaker and ESP32 in a box smaller than a matchbox.

## References

- [openai-realtime-embedded-sdk](https://github.com/openai/openai-realtime-embedded-sdk): realtime voice over WebRTC on a microcontroller
- M5Stack's [port of it](https://github.com/m5stack/openai-realtime-embedded-sdk)
- [xiaozhi-esp32](https://github.com/78/xiaozhi-esp32): an open-source ESP32 voice assistant
- Home Assistant's [$13 voice remote](https://www.home-assistant.io/voice_control/thirteen-usd-voice-remote/) on the same hardware
