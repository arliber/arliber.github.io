Inspired by the screen cutouts of the Ferrari Luce interior and by picture frames in general: a deep, wood-finish frame hosting an old 7" tablet.

A white mount has cutouts in three kinds of circle and a rounded rectangle. The rectangle shows a rotating photo; the circles show an analog clock, the date, and the next calendar or task item.

## Bonus round

- A 6×6 LED matrix driven by an ESP32 for ambient light behind the mount
- A physical knob or button to rotate images, go dark or change brightness, via the tablet's web UI

Prior art: [Frameworks, an LED wooden picture frame](https://design-milk.com/frameworks-led-wooden-picture-frame/).
